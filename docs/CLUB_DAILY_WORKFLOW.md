# RallyMonitor — Club Daily Workflow

Who does what, when, and in which screen. Keep this printed near the court
or pinned in the club chat.

## Morning (before training)

**Athlete** (~1 min, from phone)
1. Open RallyMonitor → the day card is the first screen.
2. Fill the morning questionnaire: sleep, fatigue, soreness, pain.
3. If sharp pain / new injury symptoms — tell the coach in person too;
   the app flags it, but personal contact stays the rule.

**Coach** (~2–3 min, on phone)
1. Open `/coach` — "Сегодня".
2. Priority center (top): who has an acute signal, who missed the
   questionnaire, who has post-training pending, who is all good.
3. Tap a name → profile → "Сегодня" tab → see today's score vs personal
   baseline (never read an old score as today's — the date is shown).
4. Decide: normal load / reduced load / individual plan / rest day.

## Before training (~30–60 s)

**Coach**
1. `/coach/attendance` → pick group (and date, if catching up).
2. Tap per athlete: был / не был / уважительная.
3. Whole group present? "Все были" → confirm the dialog.
4. Check the "не отмечено: N" badge — should reach 0.
5. Quick look at athletes with restrictions before warm-up.

## After training

**Athlete** (~1 min)
1. Post-training questionnaire: RPE (1–10), duration, how the body feels.
2. Report any pain honestly — it changes the load plan, not punishment.

**Coach** (~2 min)
1. `/coach` → pending post-training questionnaires are shown.
2. `/coach/alerts` → severity strip; open priority signals first.
3. If needed: profile → "Заметки и события" → record a decision/note.

## Weekly (~15 min)

**Coach**
1. `/analytics`: данные (coverage) → нагрузка (14-day wave, ACWR context)
   → боль/OSTRC → сравнение.
2. Profile "Нагрузка и восстановление" for flagged athletes.
3. `/coach/report` — build a weekly group report if needed.
4. Attendance rate per athlete (profile header).

**Admin** (~10 min)
1. `/admin` — Needs Attention: athletes without group, groups without
   schedule.
2. `/admin/users` — access changes; `/admin/audit` — who did what.
3. `/admin/config` — features/rules/OSTRC zones/tests when needed.

## Rules of thumb

- No questionnaire ≠ "bad condition" — it means "no data". Ask the athlete.
- Acute pain signal always outranks a good composite score.
- Post-training wellness is a separate measure from morning readiness.
